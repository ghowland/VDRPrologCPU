AC14|Fermi paradox|given the vast number of stars and planets, and the age of the universe, why have we not detected extraterrestrial intelligence? many proposed solutions: rare Earth (habitable planets rare), great filter (civilizations self-destruct), distance/time (too far apart or asynchronous), we are not looking correctly, zoo hypothesis, etc.; Drake equation: N = R* × f_p × n_e × f_l × f_i × f_c × L (estimates number of communicating civilizations)|astrobiology
