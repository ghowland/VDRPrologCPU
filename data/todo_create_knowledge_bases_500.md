This is the large item. Each host KB with facts needs an embedding matrix: rows × d_model × 2 bytes (i16).

Some KBs are smaller (domestic, culture at ~150 rows), some larger (programming, reference at ~300 rows). The 429 MB estimate uses the average.

### Fits on Target Hardware

The Dell Legion 5 with 16-32 GB RAM runs this with headroom. The global arena at 556 MB is well under the 1 GB allocation. At 8 cores the full system is ~2.4 GB — roughly 15% of 16 GB RAM, leaving the rest for the OS, the game engine frontend, and disk cache.

### Comparison Point

A conventional 8B parameter LLM at fp16 requires ~16 GB just for weights — no KV cache, no inference scratch, no session state. It cannot decompose, inspect, edit, or selectively load any portion of those weights.

VDR-Prolog at 500 domains with 107K addressable data entries, 115K typed relations, full provenance chains, millisecond temporal data, self-referencing addresses, and per-query GEMM scoping: **556 MB**. Roughly 29× less memory than the smallest practical conventional LLM, with every byte inspectable and editable.

### Generation Cost

At the demonstrated rate of 59 KBs in 4 hours using LLM-assisted compaction: 500 KBs ≈ **34 hours**. A long weekend of generation, then the conventional LLM is no longer needed for serving.
